Cultural Festivals

  • Diwali: Festival of lights celebrated across India.
  • Holi: Festival of colors and happiness.
  • Durga Puja: A major cultural festival in West Bengal.
  • Pongal: Harvest festival celebrated in Tamil Nadu.
  • Onam: Traditional harvest festival of Kerala.

Famous Indian Cuisine

North India

Chole Bhature, Paratha and Butter Chicken.

South India

Dosa, Idli, Sambar and Hyderabadi Biryani.

East India

Rasgulla, Macher Jhol and traditional sweets.

West India

Dhokla, Pav Bhaji and Goan seafood dishes.

Travel Tips

  • Plan your trip and book accommodation in advance.
  • Carry identification and essential travel documents.
  • Respect local customs, traditions and religious places.
  • Keep your belongings safe in crowded places.
  • Check weather conditions before travelling.
  • Keep tourist destinations clean and avoid littering.
